Unbarred Tight SpiralSpiral arms wound tightly around the core, no bar.
Unbarred Loose SpiralSpiral arms wound loosely in an open pattern, no bar.

Honest note: this model was trained on a small 2,500-image subset (12 epochs, CPU only) and scores 48.00% test accuracy on 375 test images. It is strongest on edge-on galaxies and weakest on disturbed ones — and a photo that is not a galaxy at all will still get a label, which will be meaningless. Treat every prediction as a guess with a confidence score, not a fact.
